The identity of repath: one mark, one name, two colours. This page is the whole of it.

01The mark

A lowercase r whose arm is a step response: the signal rises, overshoots, rings and settles. It is the picture a circuit simulator exists to draw, and it is the first letter of the name. One stroke, nothing else.

02Construction

The mark is drawn on a grid of 128 units. The stroke is 7 units wide with round caps and round joins. The stem stands at x = 36 and the arm settles at y = 43, a third of the way down from the first peak. Never redraw it: take the path from the files.

03Colour

Two colours, and they mean something: Signal blue is the response while it moves, Settled green is where it comes to rest. The stroke runs from one to the other, left to right. Each has a darker twin for light grounds. Everything else is ink, paper and night.

04The name

Always repath, lowercase, one word — also at the start of a sentence. Set in Geist, weight 600, tracking −0.035 em. In the logo files it is outlines, so nothing needs the font.

05The logo

The mark and the name on one line. The mark stands on the baseline of the name and is 1.16 times as tall as the h; the space between them is 0.15 em of the type. The two are never set in any other arrangement: when there is no room for the line, use the icon.

06Space and size

Keep a clear space around the logo and the icon of three stroke widths (21 units of the grid). The logo is never smaller than 20 px tall; under that, use the icon. The glow belongs to the icon at 48 px and over, on dark, and nowhere else.

At 32 px and under the stroke would thin out to less than two pixels, so the small cut takes over: the same path with a stroke of 10.5 units and no glow. The favicon is the small cut.

07Not like this

Do not turn the gradient round or change its colours.
Do not change the weight of the stroke.
Do not stretch, slant or turn it.
RePath
Do not write the name in capitals or in another type.
